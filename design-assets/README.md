## Illustration prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: bespoke wide website hero illustration for Nabda, a free tool that turns someone's Android phone and SIM into an SMS API. Create an outstanding, clean editorial black ink drawing on a flat solid warm-white background #faf9f4, landscape composition about 2:1. This is an illustration asset ONLY, not a screenshot or web page. The visual is a witty, lovingly drawn little communication workshop: at the left a small open laptop showing only a pair of curly code braces, a winding cable with a paper luggage tag leads through a small simple puffy cloud to a much larger Android phone in the center; the phone is upright, with slightly wonky physical buttons, a visible SIM card on its screen, a small stack of envelopes neatly waiting at its foot; a single paper envelope sails toward the right along a graceful dotted ink line into an open hand. The phone is the hero, roughly half the image height, hands and objects anatomically plausible but simplified editorial cartoon line art, no faces or eyes on devices. Draw with confident brush pen contours, variable stroke weight, sparse hand-hatched shadows and beautiful negative space. A couple of mint-green #c8edcb and muted peach #f0c9ad flat ink accents, rest charcoal #202824. Charming sophisticated independent developer zine, clean hand lettering sensibility but NO WORDS except the simple curly braces on laptop. All parts belong to one coherent hand-drawn scene, ground lines connect them, clear visual flow left to right, equal visual density across composition with no boxed panels. Avoid gradients, 3D, polished vector icons, stock SaaS illustration, sparkles, busy tiny doodles, childlike clipart, thick grey outlines, logos, watermark, labels, text or UI buttons. Crisp high resolution print-quality ink drawing with tasteful organic irregularity.
