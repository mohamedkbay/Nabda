Questions

Free, without vague promises.

Does the Free plan expire?

No. The plan has no trial deadline. Usage renews each calendar month within the documented limits.

Does Nabda pay for the SMS?

No. The organization provides and controls its phone, SIM, mobile plan, balance, and carrier relationship.

Are messages unlimited?

No. The Free plan includes 1,000 accepted platform operations per month and 10 per minute, plus device, contact, and webhook limits.

Can we send promotional campaigns?

The MVP is for expected transactional messages. Unsolicited advertising and attempts to bypass opt-outs, carrier rules, or platform limits are prohibited.

Start free, then verify everything in Mock.

Provision the permanent Free plan, validate authentication, queueing, usage, contacts, opt-outs, and signed events, then treat live SMS as a separate operational decision.
